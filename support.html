Do my friends need the app?

No. WeShare is a personal ledger on your device. You add friends by name, record who paid and who shared, and WeShare works out the balances. Use Send reminder or the PDF/CSV export to share a summary.

Is my data uploaded anywhere?

No. Everything stays on your device and there is no account. See the privacy policy for the details, including the anonymous diagnostics sent by Google's on-device receipt scanner.

Does "Record payment" send money?

No. It records that someone paid outside the app (cash, bank transfer or a wallet) so the balances update.

How does "simplify debts" work?

WeShare replaces a web of small debts with the fewest payments that leave everyone with exactly the same net balance. The original debts are always shown below the plan.

How do I back up or move my data?

Settings → Export → JSON backup creates a full copy you can save to Files or send to yourself. Data is not synced between devices.

How do I delete my data?

Settings → Delete all data erases everything from the device immediately. Deleting the app does the same.

Is WeShare free?

Yes. Splitting expenses, balances and settling up are free, with no ads. WeShare Plus is an optional subscription (monthly or yearly) that adds receipt scanning, reports and charts, CSV/PDF exports and recurring expenses.

How do I cancel or restore WeShare Plus?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple: open Settings → your name → Subscriptions on your iPhone, or the App Store → your account → Subscriptions. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date to avoid the next charge. On a new device, open WeShare → Profile → WeShare Plus → Restore purchases. Deleting your data in WeShare doesn't cancel the subscription. For refunds, use reportaproblem.apple.com.
