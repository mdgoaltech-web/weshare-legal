WeShare Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 October 2026

The short version: WeShare keeps your expenses on your device. We run no servers for the app, have no accounts, show no ads and do not track you. Subscriptions are handled by Apple. The only data that leaves your device automatically is anonymous diagnostics from Google's on-device text recognition, described below.

WeShare ("the app") is published by Afex Digital LLC ("we", "us").

1. Information the app stores

The name and optional phone number you enter for your profile; the names (and any optional contact details) you enter for friends; and the groups, expenses, receipt photos, notes, tags, recurring expenses and repayments you record; plus preferences such as language, theme, currency and notification settings. There is no sign-in and no password.

2. Where it is stored

All of this is stored only on your device, in the app's private storage. We do not receive it. Your friends do not receive it either: WeShare is a personal ledger, and nothing is shared unless you share it yourself (see section 5).

3. Receipt scanning and Google ML Kit

When you scan a receipt, the text is read on your device using Google ML Kit. Your receipt images and their text are never uploaded. ML Kit does send Google limited diagnostic information about the feature itself: device model and OS version, app identifier and version, a per-installation identifier, performance metrics, API configuration and error codes. This information does not include your receipts, expenses or contacts, is not linked to your identity and is not used for tracking. See Google's ML Kit terms.

4. Permissions

  • Camera and photos to scan or attach a receipt, only when you choose to.
  • Notifications for reminders you turn on in Settings.
  • Face ID / Touch ID for the optional App Lock.

WeShare never reads your address book.

5. Sharing

Data leaves WeShare only when you share it yourself: a CSV, PDF or JSON export, or a reminder message, sent through the iOS share sheet to an app and people you choose.

6. Purchases

WeShare Plus subscriptions are processed entirely by Apple. We do not receive your payment details. The app asks the App Store on your device whether your Apple ID has an active subscription; we do not store your purchase history on any server.

7. Advertising, analytics and tracking

WeShare contains no advertising, no analytics SDK and no tracking across other companies' apps or websites.

8. Your choices and deletion

You can export your data from Settings at any time. Settings → Delete all data erases your profile and every record from the device immediately. Deleting the app also removes its data. Because we hold no copies, there is nothing further for us to delete. Deleting data does not cancel a WeShare Plus subscription; manage that in your Apple ID settings.

9. Children

WeShare is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

10. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above, and show the change in the app before it takes effect.
